About ICT Department

The Information and Communication Technology Department at Government Polytechnic for Girls, Surat provides quality technical education through experienced faculty members, modern laboratories, practical learning, and industry-oriented activities. The department focuses on developing technical knowledge, professional skills, innovation, and ethical values to prepare students for higher education and successful careers in the IT industry.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a leading department in Information and Communication Technology by providing quality education, promoting innovation, technical excellence, and preparing skilled professionals for society and industry.

Mission

To provide quality technical education through experienced faculty, modern laboratories, practical learning, industry interaction, ethical values, and continuous skill development.

PEO & PSO

Program Educational Objectives (PEO)

  • Apply technical knowledge to solve real-world ICT problems.
  • Develop professional skills and teamwork abilities.
  • Continue higher education and lifelong learning.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSO)

  • Develop software applications using modern programming tools.
  • Design, manage and maintain computer networks and ICT systems.
